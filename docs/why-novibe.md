# Why NoVibe.app?

Most AI developer tools help you write code faster. Very few help you keep a project alive.

NoVibe.app exists to solve the continuity problem for solo and side-project developers: turning scattered effort into a clear weekly narrative and a simple next step that keeps momentum real.

## The problem we actually solve
- **Activity is not progress.** GitHub commits and PRs are noisy signals and often miss the real work.
- **Intentions are vague.** Without a clear "done," weekly goals become fuzzy and discouraging.
- **Momentum collapses without recovery.** Missing a week creates guilt instead of guidance.

## What NoVibe does differently
- **Continuity, not just tracking.** It creates a weekly narrative that connects intention to evidence.
- **Tiny, concrete next steps.** Each week ends with a single, low-friction action you can actually ship.
- **Low-pressure accountability.** When drift happens, NoVibe helps you recalibrate instead of punishing you.
- **Built on real code activity.** Sign in with GitHub or GitLab and anchor each week to a real repo.

## How it stops “vibe coding”
- **Evidence-backed reviews.** Weekly progress is evaluated against real signals, not just enthusiasm.
- **Concrete next steps.** The loop ends with a single action you can complete in a short session.
- **Drift detection.** If activity diverges from intent, NoVibe forces a choice: update the goal or pause it.

## The promise
NoVibe.app helps you ship small, meaningful progress every week and keeps your project moving when motivation fades.

## Enterprise value
- **Reduces stalled initiatives.** Weekly evidence + next steps make drift visible early.
- **Lightweight status signal.** Teams get concise, evidence‑backed progress without heavy PM overhead.
- **Faster context transfer.** New engineers and managers can read a project’s continuity narrative quickly.
- **Supports autonomy.** Keeps teams aligned without micromanagement.
